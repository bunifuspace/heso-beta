A better website experience, with fewer surprises.

This Cookies Policy explains how Health and Environment Solutions Organization (HESO) may use cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

Last updated: October 2026
01

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow a website to remember certain information and help it function properly.

Similar technologies may also be used to understand how a website is accessed and how visitors interact with its pages.

02

How HESO may use cookies

Cookies may be used on the HESO website for several purposes, including:

Essential

Help the website operate correctly and provide requested functionality.

Preferences

Remember certain choices or settings to make the website easier to use.

Analytics

Help us understand website usage and identify areas that could be improved.

Security

Support website security, abuse prevention, and reliable operation.

03

Third-party services

Some features of the website may rely on third-party services. These services may use their own cookies or similar technologies in accordance with their respective policies.

HESO does not control the privacy practices of external services. We recommend reviewing their policies when you interact with third-party features or links.

04

Managing cookies

Most modern web browsers allow you to control, block, or delete cookies through their settings.

You can choose to disable cookies, but some parts of the HESO website may not function as intended if certain essential cookies are blocked.

Your browser, your choice.

Cookie controls are usually available under your browser's privacy, security, or site settings.

05

How long cookies remain

Some cookies are temporary and are removed when you close your browser. Others may remain on your device for a longer period until they expire or are manually deleted.

06

Changes to this policy

HESO may update this Cookies Policy when website functionality, technology, services, or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page.
