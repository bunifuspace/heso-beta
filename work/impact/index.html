Change that can be seen, measured, and felt.

HESO works alongside communities to turn knowledge, partnerships, and practical action into meaningful improvements in health, livelihoods, and the environment.

01 Measuring What Matters

Impact starts with people.

For HESO, impact is not only about numbers. It is about whether people have better access to knowledge, healthier environments, stronger livelihoods, and greater ability to respond to challenges.

We use evidence, community feedback, monitoring, and learning to understand what is working and where more action is needed.
