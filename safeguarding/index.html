Safeguarding

People come first.

HESO is committed to creating safe, respectful, and inclusive environments for the people and communities connected to our work.

Report a concern ↗
01 Our Commitment

Everyone has the right to be treated with dignity and respect.

Safeguarding is part of how HESO works. We seek to prevent harm, abuse, exploitation, harassment, discrimination, and inappropriate conduct in our programs, activities, and interactions with communities and partners.

02 · What We Protect

Creating safer spaces for everyone.

01

Children

We are committed to protecting children from abuse, exploitation, neglect, and other forms of harm.

02

Vulnerable People

We work to reduce risks and protect people who may face increased vulnerability in the communities we serve.

03

Communities

Community members should be able to participate in our activities without fear of harm, exploitation, or abuse.

04

Staff & Volunteers

We seek to maintain respectful working environments for employees, volunteers, interns, and other team members.

03 · Our Principles

Safety is part of responsible development work.

Safeguarding is not separate from our programs. It influences how we plan activities, work with communities, engage partners, and respond when concerns are raised.

01 Respect and dignity
02 Do no harm
03 Inclusion and equality
04 Accountability
04 · Raising a Concern

Speak up when something doesn't feel right.

If you see, experience, or receive information about conduct that may place someone at risk, we encourage you to raise the concern through the appropriate HESO channel.

01

Report

Share the concern with HESO through an appropriate reporting or contact channel.

02

Listen

Concerns should be treated seriously, respectfully, and with appropriate sensitivity.

03

Respond

Appropriate action can be taken based on the nature and circumstances of the concern.

05 · Expected Conduct

Respect is everyone's responsibility.

Everyone involved in HESO activities is expected to behave professionally and respectfully and to avoid conduct that could cause harm or create an unsafe environment.

Respect people and their boundaries.
Never exploit a position of trust or authority.
Protect confidential information appropriately.
Report safeguarding concerns responsibly.
06 · Policies & Documents

Learn more about our safeguarding commitments.

More detailed safeguarding policies, procedures, and related organizational documents can be made available as they are formally adopted and published.
