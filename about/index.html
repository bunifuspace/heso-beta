Health and Environment Solutions Organization (HESO) is a non-governmental organization committed to improving community well-being and promoting sustainable development.

HESO Arusha, Tanzania
01 Who We Are

Health and Environment Solutions Organization

HESO addresses interconnected challenges in public health, environmental conservation, agriculture, water resources, sanitation, climate resilience, and sustainable livelihoods.

Through community-centered approaches, research, advocacy, education, and partnerships, we work to create healthier, more resilient communities and a more sustainable future for Tanzania.

Our Commitment

A healthier life in a clean, safe, and thriving environment.

At HESO, we are committed to building a Tanzania where every person can live a healthy life in a clean, safe, and thriving environment.

Our interdisciplinary approach brings together public health, environmental science, community engagement, research, advocacy, and sustainable development to address today's challenges while preparing communities for the future.

We believe that with the right knowledge, tools, opportunities, and partnerships, every community can become a champion of sustainable development.

03 Our Objectives

Turning commitment into action.

Our work brings health, environmental protection, community empowerment, research, and sustainable development together.

01

Promote Public Health and Disease Prevention

To improve community health through awareness campaigns, access to health information and services, and the promotion of preventive practices targeting both communicable and non-communicable diseases.

02

Support Environmental Conservation and Climate Resilience

To promote the protection and sustainable management of natural resources while supporting climate adaptation, mitigation, and actions that reduce harmful environmental impacts.

03

Advance Community Education and Empowerment

To educate and empower individuals and communities, particularly women, youth, and vulnerable populations, on sustainable practices related to health, hygiene, waste management, environmental conservation, and community development.

04

Facilitate Research and Innovation

To conduct and support locally relevant research that contributes to improved health and environmental policies, technologies, and practices suited to Tanzania's unique context.

05

Strengthen Policy Advocacy and Stakeholder Engagement

To collaborate with government institutions, civil society organizations, academic institutions, development partners, and international organizations to promote effective policies, mobilize resources, and advance integrated action on health and environmental challenges.

06

Enhance Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene Services

To improve access to safe water, adequate sanitation facilities, and hygiene education, particularly in underserved and rural communities, in order to reduce disease burden and environmental degradation.

07

Promote Sustainable Livelihoods and a Green Economy

To support income-generating activities, sustainable enterprises, and green technologies that protect the environment while strengthening community resilience, economic independence, and livelihoods.

08

Encourage Eco-Entrepreneurship and Skills Development

To promote training and initiatives that equip community members with practical skills in sustainable practices, including organic farming, renewable energy, waste recycling, and environmentally friendly crafts.

04

HESO Preamble

Recognizing the vital connection between public health and a sustainable environment, and the unique health and environmental challenges facing the United Republic of Tanzania, HESO is committed to promoting integrated, locally driven solutions.

Guided by the values of equity, sustainability, integrity, innovation, and community empowerment, we seek to work in partnership with government institutions, local communities, academic institutions, civil society organizations, and international stakeholders.

Through public education, scientific research, advocacy, partnerships, and evidence-based action, we aim to protect Tanzania's natural heritage, improve the health and well-being of its people, strengthen community resilience, and promote sustainable development for present and future generations.

Our Vision

A healthy, sustainable, and environmentally resilient Tanzania where all people thrive in harmony with nature.

We envision empowered communities that actively participate in protecting and sustainably managing their natural resources while building resilience to climate change and other environmental challenges.

Our Mission

To promote the health and environmental well-being of Tanzanian communities through innovative, evidence-based solutions, education, research, policy advocacy, partnerships, and sustainable development initiatives.

HESO is committed to empowering communities, strengthening public health systems, protecting ecosystems, and promoting sustainable livelihoods to improve the quality of life for present and future generations.
