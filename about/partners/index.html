Our Partners

Progress is stronger when we work together.

HESO believes meaningful and lasting change comes through collaboration. We work to build relationships that connect communities, knowledge, resources, and practical action.

01 Working Together

Different strengths. A shared purpose.

The challenges facing communities are connected and often require more than one perspective. HESO seeks to collaborate with organizations and individuals who bring different forms of knowledge, experience, resources, and expertise.

Our partnerships are built around shared objectives, responsible collaboration, and a commitment to creating meaningful value for communities.

02 · Collaboration

Connecting ideas, people, and action.

HESO seeks opportunities to work across sectors and disciplines to address health, environmental, social, and development challenges.

We value partnerships that are respectful, transparent, locally relevant, and focused on practical outcomes.

05 · Partnership Principles

Relationships built on trust.

Shared Purpose

We look for areas where our goals and capabilities can contribute to a meaningful common objective.

Respect

We value different perspectives, expertise, experiences, and forms of knowledge.

Transparency

We aim for clear communication, responsible decision-making, and accountability throughout our work.

Long-Term Thinking

We seek relationships that can create lasting value rather than focusing only on short-term activities.
