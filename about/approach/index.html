Our Approach

Listen first. Work together. Build for the long term.

HESO takes a community-centered approach to health, environmental protection, and sustainable development.

01 How We Work

Solutions work better when people help shape them.

HESO works alongside communities and partners to understand local challenges and identify practical responses.

Our approach brings together community knowledge, evidence, education, research, partnerships, and action — while keeping local priorities at the centre.

02 · Our Process

A practical way of turning challenges into action.

01

Listen

We begin by listening to communities, understanding their experiences, priorities, knowledge, and concerns.

02

Understand

We look at the wider factors behind a challenge, using available evidence, research, local knowledge, and stakeholder perspectives.

03

Collaborate

We bring communities, institutions, professionals, and partners together to develop responses that reflect local realities.

04

Act

We support practical initiatives, education, advocacy, research, and other activities that address identified needs.

05

Learn

We reflect on what works, what does not, and what can be improved so that future action becomes more effective.

06

Build Forward

We focus on approaches that can strengthen community resilience and contribute to lasting, sustainable change.

03 · Community First

Start with people, not assumptions.

Every community has its own circumstances, knowledge, strengths, and priorities. HESO seeks to understand these realities before deciding what action is appropriate.

Community participation helps ensure that initiatives are relevant, respectful, practical, and more likely to create lasting value.

04 · Evidence & Learning

Good decisions need good information.

HESO promotes the use of evidence, research, monitoring, community knowledge, and lessons from implementation to strengthen decision-making.

We aim to learn continuously and use what we learn to improve programs, inform advocacy, and support practical solutions.

06 · Our Principles

How we want the work to feel.

Inclusive

We value participation and seek to include different voices, especially those closest to the challenges being addressed.

Evidence-based

We use research, available evidence, experience, and local knowledge to inform our decisions.

Practical

We focus on solutions that respond to real needs and can be applied within local contexts.

Sustainable

We consider long-term environmental, social, and economic outcomes rather than short-term results alone.

Collaborative

We believe stronger outcomes come from working with others and learning from different perspectives.

Accountable

We aim to act responsibly, communicate openly, and remain accountable to the people and partners we work with.
