Health and environment are part of the same story.

HESO was built around a simple idea: healthier communities depend on a healthier environment.

01 Where We Begin

Different challenges. One connected reality.

Communities do not experience health, environmental, and development challenges separately. Unsafe water can affect health. Environmental degradation can affect livelihoods. Climate change can increase pressure on food, water, and household income.

HESO was established with this connection in mind — bringing health, environmental protection, education, research, and sustainable development closer together.

Health · Environment · Community
02 · What We Believe

Local knowledge belongs at the centre of development.

HESO believes communities should not simply be recipients of development efforts. They should have a voice in identifying challenges, shaping solutions, and building a future that works for them.

Our work therefore focuses on listening, learning, collaborating, and supporting practical solutions that can make a lasting difference.

04 · From Ideas to Action

Good intentions need practical work.

HESO brings together community engagement, education, research, advocacy, partnerships, and practical initiatives to respond to challenges in ways that are relevant to local communities.

We are continuing to build partnerships, learn from the communities we work with, and develop approaches that can contribute to sustainable development in Tanzania.
