01 Why Give

Small contributions can support meaningful community work.

HESO works with communities to address challenges affecting health, the environment, livelihoods, water, sanitation, agriculture, and climate resilience.

Your contribution helps create opportunities to reach more people and support practical, locally relevant solutions.
