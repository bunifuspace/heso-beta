01 Intern With HESO

Experience beyond the classroom.

An internship with HESO can provide an opportunity to connect academic learning with practical work. Interns may support ongoing activities, learn from experienced team members, participate in field activities, and develop professional skills.

02 · Areas of Learning

Find a place to learn and contribute.

01

Public Health

Learn about community health activities, health education, prevention, and program support.

02

Environment

Gain practical exposure to environmental activities, conservation, awareness, and sustainability.

03

WASH

Support work related to water, sanitation, hygiene, community awareness, and field activities.

04

Agriculture

Explore sustainable agriculture, food systems, community practices, and agricultural development.

05

Climate Resilience

Learn about climate-related challenges, adaptation, resilience, and community responses.

06

Sustainable Livelihoods

Understand approaches that support skills, opportunity, income generation, and resilient communities.

03 · The Experience

From learning to real-world experience.

Depending on the placement, interns may contribute to research, field activities, documentation, communications, data collection, community engagement, administration, or other areas connected to HESO's work.

01 Practical experience
02 Professional exposure
03 Team collaboration
04 Community experience
04 · Who Can Apply

For people ready to learn.

Students

Students looking to complement their studies with practical experience.

Graduates

Recent graduates seeking professional exposure and experience in development work.

Researchers

Individuals undertaking academic or field-based research connected to HESO's areas of work.

05 · How It Works

Start with a conversation.

01

Apply

Share your academic background, interests, skills, and internship requirements.

02

Review

HESO reviews applications based on current opportunities, needs, and areas of work.

03

Placement

Where suitable, interns are connected with an appropriate team or area of work.

Opportunities depend on our current work.

Internship availability, duration, location, and responsibilities may vary depending on HESO's programs and current capacity. Submitting an application does not guarantee placement.
