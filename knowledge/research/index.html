Evidence that helps communities move forward.

HESO uses research, community knowledge, and evidence to better understand local challenges and develop practical solutions for healthier people and a more sustainable environment.

01 Why Research Matters

Good decisions start with understanding.

Communities face different challenges, and solutions that work in one place may not work in another. HESO supports research that helps us understand local realities, identify opportunities, and make better-informed decisions.

We connect evidence with practical action so that research can contribute to programs, policies, education, and community development.

02 · Field Research

Start with the people and places we serve.

Our approach values information gathered directly from communities and the environments in which they live.

Through surveys, interviews, observation, community engagement, data collection, and other appropriate methods, we seek to understand problems from the ground up.

03 · Research Areas

Questions connected to everyday life.

01

02

Environment

Understanding environmental pressures and opportunities for conservation and sustainable resource management.

03

WASH

Studying water, sanitation, hygiene, and their relationship with health and environmental conditions.

04

Agriculture

Supporting knowledge around sustainable farming, food systems, productivity, and resilient communities.

05

Examining how communities can prepare for and respond to changing environmental and climate conditions.

06

Livelihoods

Exploring opportunities that strengthen economic resilience, skills, sustainable enterprises, and community livelihoods.

04 · Our Approach

Research that leads somewhere.

We aim to move beyond collecting information. Findings should help communities, organizations, and decision-makers understand what is happening and identify realistic ways forward.

01

Understand

Identify local needs, challenges, and opportunities.

02

Learn

Collect and analyze relevant evidence and community knowledge.

03

Share

Communicate findings in ways people and organizations can use.

04

Act

Use evidence to strengthen programs, decisions, and solutions.
