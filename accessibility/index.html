Accessibility

A website for everyone.

HESO is committed to making its website and digital information accessible, understandable, and usable by as many people as possible.

Report an accessibility issue ↗
01 Our Commitment

Information should be open and usable.

We aim to design HESO's digital experiences so that people with different abilities, devices, technologies, and ways of accessing information can use them effectively.

Accessibility is an ongoing process. We continue to review and improve the website as new content, features, and services are added.

02 · What We Aim For

Simple, clear, and easier to use.

01

Clear Content

We aim to use clear language, meaningful headings, descriptive links, and structured content that is easier to understand.

02

Keyboard Access

Website functions should be usable without requiring a mouse wherever practical.

03

Readable Design

We consider text size, spacing, contrast, layout, and visual hierarchy when creating digital experiences.

04

Alternative Text

Images and visual information are provided with alternative descriptions where appropriate.

05

Responsive Experience

We design pages to work across different screen sizes and commonly used devices.

06

Assistive Technology

We aim to support visitors who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification, and other assistive technologies.

03 · Using This Website

We want the basics to work well.

Our website is designed with accessibility in mind, but some content or third-party services may not provide the same level of accessibility.

01 Use headings to move through pages.
02 Use keyboard navigation where supported.
03 Use browser zoom or device accessibility settings.
04 Contact us if something prevents access.
04 · Ongoing Improvement

We know accessibility is never finished.

Some older content, documents, photographs, embedded services, or third-party resources may not yet meet every accessibility need. We work to identify and improve these areas as part of maintaining the website.

If you encounter a barrier, your feedback can help us understand what needs attention.

05 · Tell Us

Something not working for you?

Tell us what you were trying to access, what made it difficult, and, where possible, the page or feature involved. We will use the information to help improve the experience.
