Privacy Policy

Your information deserves care.

This Privacy Policy explains how Health and Environment Solutions Organization (HESO) collects, uses, protects, and handles information provided through our website.

Last updated: October 2026

01

Information we collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when using HESO's website, including when you contact us, apply to volunteer or intern, submit an inquiry, or communicate with our organization.

Information you may provide

  • Name and contact details
  • Email address and telephone number
  • Information submitted through applications
  • Messages and inquiries sent to HESO
  • Information provided when supporting our work

We may also collect limited technical information when you visit our website, such as browser type, device information, approximate location, pages visited, and basic usage data.

02

How we use information

Information collected through the website may be used to:

  • Respond to questions and requests
  • Process volunteer, internship, or participation applications
  • Communicate with applicants, partners, supporters, and stakeholders
  • Coordinate HESO activities and programs
  • Improve the website and user experience
  • Maintain website security and prevent misuse
  • Meet applicable legal and administrative requirements

We do not collect personal information simply for the purpose of selling it to third parties.

03

Information sharing

HESO does not routinely sell or rent personal information. Information may be shared where reasonably necessary to provide a requested service, operate the website, coordinate an activity, comply with legal obligations, or protect the rights and safety of HESO, website users, or other persons.

Where third-party services are used to process information, we expect those services to handle information in accordance with their own privacy and security practices.

04

Cookies and similar technologies

HESO may use cookies or similar technologies to support the operation, security, and performance of the website.

Some cookies may help us understand how visitors use the website so that we can improve its content and functionality. Where applicable, you can control cookies through your browser settings or available website preferences.

05

Keeping information secure

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no website, electronic transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

06

How long we keep information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain appropriate records, resolve issues, or comply with applicable legal and organizational requirements.

07

Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning the personal information we hold about you. These may include the ability to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain information.

If you have a question about information you have provided to HESO or wish to make a privacy-related request, contact us using the details below.

08

External websites

The HESO website may contain links to external websites, organizations, platforms, or resources. These websites are not controlled by HESO and may have their own privacy policies.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy practices of external websites before submitting personal information.

09

Changes to this policy

HESO may update this Privacy Policy when our website, activities, services, or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page together with the relevant revision date.

10 · Contact

Questions about privacy?

If you have questions, concerns, or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or the information you have provided, please contact HESO.
