Agriculture & Livelihoods

Growing healthier communities through sustainable agriculture.

HESO promotes practical and sustainable agricultural approaches that strengthen livelihoods, support food security, protect natural resources, and help communities adapt to a changing climate.

01 Why Agriculture Matters

Healthy land supports healthy lives.

Agriculture is closely connected to food, health, income, water, climate, and the environment. When natural resources are managed responsibly, communities can build stronger and more resilient livelihoods.

HESO works to encourage agricultural practices that respond to local needs while protecting the resources communities depend on.

03 · Community-led

Local knowledge is part of the solution.

Farmers and communities have valuable knowledge built through generations of experience. HESO seeks to combine this local knowledge with practical information, research, and appropriate technologies.

Our approach focuses on learning with communities rather than simply bringing solutions to them.

04 · Sustainable Practices

Small changes can protect resources for generations.

Sustainable agriculture can take many forms depending on the local environment, available resources, and needs of farmers.

Organic & Responsible Farming Encouraging practices that support healthy soils and responsible production.
Agroforestry Integrating trees and crops in ways that can benefit farms and ecosystems.
Water Conservation Promoting careful management of water resources in agricultural areas.
Waste Reduction Exploring ways to reduce agricultural waste and make better use of available resources.
05 · Livelihoods

From sustainable farming to stronger livelihoods.

Agriculture supports many households across Tanzania. HESO seeks to promote approaches that can strengthen economic resilience while reducing pressure on the environment.

Skills

Practical knowledge that can help individuals and communities improve sustainable production.

Enterprise

Encouraging opportunities around sustainable agricultural products and green businesses.

Youth

Creating opportunities for young people to participate in agriculture, innovation, and sustainable enterprise.

Women

Supporting inclusive opportunities that strengthen participation and economic independence.

06 · Connected Challenges

Agriculture does not exist on its own.

Food production depends on healthy soil, reliable water, biodiversity, climate conditions, and healthy communities.

That is why HESO connects agricultural work with environmental conservation, public health, WASH, climate resilience, and sustainable livelihoods.
