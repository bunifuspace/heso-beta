WASH

Safe water. Better sanitation. Healthier communities.

HESO works to promote access to safe water, adequate sanitation, and healthy hygiene practices, particularly in communities where these services remain limited.

01 Why WASH Matters

Water, sanitation, and hygiene are foundations of good health.

Access to safe water and sanitation affects everyday life — from drinking and preparing food to personal hygiene, schools, households, and community spaces.

HESO promotes practical WASH solutions that support healthier communities while encouraging responsible management of water and environmental resources.

03 · Community-led

Better WASH starts with understanding local needs.

Communities understand their own water, sanitation, and hygiene challenges. HESO seeks to work alongside them to identify priorities and support practical responses.

Education, participation, local knowledge, and partnerships are central to creating solutions that can continue beyond individual interventions.

04 · Health & Prevention

Prevention begins with everyday choices.

Clean water, safe sanitation, and good hygiene practices can play an important role in protecting community health.

HESO supports health education that helps people understand practical ways to reduce risks in homes, schools, workplaces, and community settings.

05 · Water & Environment

Protecting water means protecting the environment around it.

Water resources are connected to ecosystems, agriculture, livelihoods, and community health. HESO therefore considers environmental protection an important part of WASH work.

Water Protection Promoting responsible use and protection of water resources.
Waste Management Encouraging practices that reduce environmental contamination.
Clean Communities Supporting healthier and cleaner shared environments.
Climate Resilience Considering changing environmental conditions and water security.
