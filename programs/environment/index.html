Programs · Environment

Protecting the places communities depend on.

HESO promotes practical environmental action that protects natural resources, reduces environmental harm, and helps communities build resilience to a changing climate.

01 Our Focus

A healthier environment supports healthier communities.

The health of people and the health of their surroundings are closely connected. HESO works with communities and partners to address environmental challenges in ways that are practical, locally relevant, and sustainable.

03 · On the Ground

Environmental work starts with understanding local realities.

Communities experience environmental challenges differently. Our approach begins by listening to local knowledge, identifying practical needs, and working with people to develop solutions that can last.

04 · Why It Matters

Environmental challenges are community challenges.

Pollution, changing weather patterns, loss of natural resources, unsafe waste practices, and environmental degradation can affect health, food security, livelihoods, and quality of life.

HESO connects environmental action with the wider needs of communities so that solutions can create benefits beyond the environment itself.
