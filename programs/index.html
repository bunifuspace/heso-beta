Turning community needs into practical action.

HESO works across health, environment, water, agriculture, climate resilience, education, research, and sustainable livelihoods to support stronger communities across Tanzania.

01 What We Do

Connected challenges need connected solutions.

Health, the environment, livelihoods, water, and climate are closely connected. Our programs bring these areas together and focus on solutions that can make a practical difference in people's everyday lives.

01

Public Health

Promoting health awareness, disease prevention, healthy practices, and access to reliable health information within communities.

Explore program ↗
02

Environment and Conservation

Supporting conservation, responsible resource management, pollution reduction, and healthier environments.

Explore program ↗
03

Sustainable Agriculture

Encouraging sustainable farming practices that strengthen food security, protect natural resources, and improve livelihoods.

Explore program ↗
04

Water, Sanitation and Hygiene

Working to improve access to safe water, sanitation facilities, and practical hygiene education, particularly in underserved communities.

Explore program ↗
05

Education and Empowerment

Equipping communities, women, youth, and vulnerable groups with knowledge and practical skills for positive change.

Explore program ↗
06

Climate Resilience

Supporting communities to understand, prepare for, and respond to climate-related challenges.

Explore program ↗
07

Research and Innovation

Supporting locally relevant research and evidence-based approaches that can inform better health, environmental, and development decisions.

Explore program ↗
08

Sustainable Livelihoods

Supporting green enterprises, skills development, and income-generating opportunities that strengthen community resilience.

Explore program ↗
