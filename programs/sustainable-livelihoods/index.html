Sustainable Livelihoods

Stronger livelihoods. More resilient communities.

HESO supports people and communities to develop practical skills, sustainable income opportunities, and environmentally responsible enterprises that can strengthen economic resilience.

01 Why Livelihoods Matter

A healthy community needs opportunities to thrive.

Livelihoods are connected to health, food security, education, the environment, and the ability of families to respond to unexpected challenges.

HESO promotes approaches that help people build skills, explore sustainable economic opportunities, and strengthen their ability to support themselves and their communities.

03 · Community-led

People know what their communities need.

Sustainable livelihoods begin with understanding the people, resources, markets, skills, and opportunities that already exist within a community.

HESO works alongside communities to identify practical opportunities and strengthen local capacity rather than relying on one-size-fits-all solutions.

04 · Green Economy

Economic growth can work with nature.

Communities depend on healthy land, water, forests, and other natural resources. Sustainable enterprise can create economic opportunities while helping protect these resources.

Sustainable Agriculture Supporting productive approaches that protect soil, water, and ecosystems.
Recycling & Waste Exploring opportunities that turn waste challenges into useful resources.
Eco-friendly Products Encouraging responsible production using locally available resources.
Renewable Energy Promoting awareness of cleaner technologies and energy opportunities.
